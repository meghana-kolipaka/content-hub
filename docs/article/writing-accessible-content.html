Writing Accessible Web Content

By Meghana K. · 2026-10-09 · Content

Practical habits such as alt text, clear headings and descriptive links that make content usable for everyone.

Accessible content works for people using screen readers, keyboards and small screens.

Use one main heading per page, keep headings in order, and write alt text that describes the purpose of an image.

Link text should make sense alone. Prefer 'read the style guide' over 'click here'.
