Why Metadata Matters for Findability

By Meghana K. · 2026-10-14 · Content

Titles, descriptions and tags help search engines and colleagues find the right content quickly.

Metadata is information about content: title, summary, author, date, tags and status.

Consistent metadata lets teams search, filter and update many pages at once.

Pair this with Writing Accessible Web Content to keep pages both findable and usable.
